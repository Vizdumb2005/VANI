The Problem (20% Weight)

The ₹10 Lakh Crore Infrastructure Misalignment Challenge

Billions in public capital are spent every fiscal year, yet unaddressed gaps persist while citizen feedback remains severely fragmented.

1. Linguistic Exclusion

Over 85% of Indian citizens cannot articulate complex technical grievances in written English or formal Hindi. Voice is the universal medium.

2. Fragmented Silos

Petitions live across siloed CM helplines, municipal registers, and WhatsApp without centralized geospatial synthesis.

3. Speculative Allocation

Capital is frequently sanctioned based on political visibility rather than empirical demand intensity and multidimensional deprivation indices.

4. No Verification Loop

Governments lack automated counterfactual mechanisms to verify if completed projects actually silenced citizen complaints.
