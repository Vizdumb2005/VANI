# Official Submission Brief — VAANI

**Project Name:** VAANI (Voice-to-Network Aggregated National Intelligence)  
**Track:** Public Sector Innovation / Digital Public Goods  
**Submission Requirement:** 2–3 Line Brief Description (for Hackathon submission portal)

---

### Official 2–3 Line Submission Description:

> **VAANI is an open-source Digital Public Good powered by Google Gemini AI that aggregates citizen infrastructure grievances via voice, text, and photos across 22 scheduled Indian languages. It verifies physical damage with Gemini Multimodal Vision, fuses citizen demand with Census 2011 and NFHS-5 data to prioritize public spending through explainable MCDA, and validates real-world delivery impact using Synthetic Control Methods.**

---

### Key Highlights for Judges:
* **Google AI Integration:** Google Gemini 2.0/1.5 Multimodal Vision (damage verification) & Gemini GenAI Policy Agent (automated Cabinet memos).
* **Scale & Reach:** 22 scheduled Indian languages, 55+ districts, 131 demand hotspots anchored to Local Government Directory (LGD) codes.
* **Closed Policy Loop:** Causal Synthetic Control Method (SCM) measuring 40%–67% demand decay post-infrastructure completion with placebo validation ($p \le 0.10$).
* **Cross-Border Scalable:** Modular BRICS adaptability across India (LGD), Brazil (IBGE), and South Africa (MDB).
* **Statutory Compliance:** Certified compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (zero audio retention, salted SHA-256 pseudonyms, $k \ge 3$ cell suppression).
