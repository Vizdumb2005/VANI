## 2. Platform Purpose & Important Disclaimers

### 2.1 Not an Emergency Response Service
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **VAANI IS NOT AN EMERGENCY DISPATCH SERVICE.**
> VAANI is designed for developmental, spatial, and public infrastructure planning (roads, water supply, electricity grids, primary healthcare infrastructure, sanitation).
> In case of immediate life safety, crime, fire, or acute medical emergencies, citizens must immediately contact national emergency numbers:
> - **112**: All-in-One National Emergency Services
> - **100**: Police
> - **101**: Fire Services
> - **108 / 102**: Ambulance & Emergency Medical Services

### 2.2 Decision-Support Mechanism for Policymakers
The Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) priority scores and Synthetic Control Method (SCM) impact models are advisory tools designed to support evidence-based public budgeting. They do not replace statutory administrative approvals, public procurement protocols, or constitutional administrative authority.

## 3. Acceptable Use Policy

Citizens and institutional users agree to the following terms:

### 3.1 Permitted Uses
- Submitting genuine citizen grievances or feedback regarding public infrastructure in any of India's 22 scheduled languages.
- Querying public demand hotspots and aggregated spatial infrastructure priorities for academic, governmental, or civic monitoring purposes.
- Integrating public OpenAPI 3.1 endpoints into public governance dashboards or civil society tracking tools.

### 3.2 Prohibited Conduct
Users must NOT:
- Submit spam, fraudulent, defamatory, sexually explicit, abusive, or knowingly falsified claims.
- Attempt to launch denial-of-service (DoS) or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks against the API.
- Attempt to de-anonymize citizen pseudonyms or link aggregated demand hotspots back to individual private identities.
- Misrepresent affiliation with official government entities without authorization.
